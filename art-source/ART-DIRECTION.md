# Iron Field — Taiwanese Temple Fantasy Art Direction

## Art Style Identity

**Style Name:** Taiwanese Temple Fantasy

A highly stylized fantasy action-game aesthetic inspired by Taiwanese traditional temple architecture, folk festivals, religious decorative craftsmanship, and East Asian heroic mythology.

The visual style combines:

- Taiwanese temple ornamentation and traditional craftsmanship
- Colorful folk-festival aesthetics
- Stylized martial-arts fantasy
- Hand-painted, anime-inspired action RPG illustration
- Ornate, premium-quality fantasy game interfaces

The overall atmosphere should feel **heroic, energetic, mystical, festive, and spectacular**.

It should evoke the feeling of an epic battle taking place in a fantastical world inspired by Taiwan's temple culture.

## Visual Language

### Character Design

Characters should have:

- Strong, instantly recognizable silhouettes
- Stylized heroic proportions
- Expressive faces and dynamic combat poses
- Elaborate armor inspired by Taiwanese temple guardians and traditional ceremonial costumes
- Decorative metalwork, jade ornaments, red tassels, embroidered fabrics, and flowing ribbons
- High visual detail concentrated around faces, shoulders, weapons, and important accessories

Differentiate factions and classes using shape language and color.

### Materials and Textures

Use:

- Aged bronze and antique gold
- Dark iron and engraved steel
- Lacquered red surfaces
- Carved jade and turquoise gemstones
- Embroidered silk and fabric
- Traditional wood carving
- Ceramic mosaic and decorative relief patterns

Materials should look painterly and richly textured rather than photorealistic.

### Ornamentation

Use decorative patterns inspired by:

- Taiwanese temple roof sculptures and Jian Nian (剪黏)
- Traditional temple door paintings
- Stone guardian lions
- Dragon and phoenix motifs
- Auspicious cloud patterns
- Geometric wooden latticework
- Traditional festival banners and tassels

These details should reinforce the visual identity without overwhelming gameplay readability.

### Color Palette

Primary palette:

- Vermilion red: `#B52B2B`
- Antique gold: `#D9A441`
- Peacock teal: `#087F80`
- Dark charcoal: `#17191C`
- Warm ivory: `#F4E3BF`

Use gold as a decorative accent, red for aggressive combat elements, and teal/cyan for magical or ranged abilities.

### Illustration and Rendering

- Stylized 2D fantasy game illustration
- Painterly shading with crisp, controlled linework
- Strong lighting contrast
- Vibrant but disciplined color palette
- Detailed focal subjects with simplified secondary elements
- Clear readable silhouettes
- Dynamic flowing cloud and energy effects
- Premium action RPG concept-art quality

Avoid photorealism and generic stock fantasy visuals.

## UI Design

Design a consistent game interface using:

- Ornate gold borders
- Dark textured panels
- Decorative cloud-shaped corners
- Red and teal selection states
- Traditional lattice patterns
- Subtle lacquer and brushed-metal textures

Use restrained decoration for in-game HUD elements.

Reserve highly ornate frames for menus, character selection, relic selection, and reward screens.

## Gameplay Asset Requirements

The project uses Babylon.js for a real-time browser-based Musou game.

Assets must remain practical for rendering and integration.

- Generate transparent PNGs or WebP images for inventory icons, skills, effects, and portraits.
- Prefer 512×512 source icons, with smaller optimized runtime variants.
- Maintain consistent padding, orientation, and visual scale.
- Never bake labels or statistics into icons.
- Use separate assets for distinct game objects.
- Optimize runtime textures and avoid excessive draw calls.
- Preserve gameplay clarity when hundreds of enemies are visible.

For 3D assets, use low-poly or stylized meshes with temple-inspired materials and ornaments.

Keep decorative geometry lightweight and avoid unnecessary high-poly details.

## Art Direction Constraints

**Do:**
- Express a distinctly Taiwan-inspired fantasy identity.
- Prioritize gameplay readability.
- Maintain consistency between heroes, enemies, weapons, UI, and effects.
- Use detailed decoration selectively.
- Create original characters and symbols.

**Avoid:**
- Generic European medieval fantasy armor.
- Japanese samurai or ninja aesthetics as the primary identity.
- Generic Chinese palace aesthetics without specifically Taiwanese influences.
- Excessive gold filigree that makes everything visually identical.
- Overly realistic textures.
- Visual clutter during large battles.
- Direct reproductions of sacred religious figures or temple artwork.

## Desired Emotional Experience

Players should feel like they are controlling legendary warriors in a spectacular Taiwanese folk-fantasy battlefield.

Combat should feel powerful, colorful, dramatic, and immediately satisfying.

The world should feel culturally distinctive, handcrafted, and memorable.

**Creative principle: Taiwanese temple artistry meets modern Musou action-game spectacle.**
